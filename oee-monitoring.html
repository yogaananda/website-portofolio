The Efficiency Goal

Translating raw machine downtime and cycle counts into actionable performance metrics.

The challenge was integrating diverse PLC outputs into a unified web interface. By implementing a high-frequency polling service, we provided floor managers with a live visual of Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE). The system identifies micro-stoppages that were previously invisible, leading to a significant increase in production throughput.
