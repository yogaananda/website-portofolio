The Mission

Organizing chaos into a hierarchical system of priorities and resolution paths.

Legacy email-based support causes significant delays and lost requests. We built a custom ticketing portal that automatically categorizes issues, assigns them to the correct specialist, and tracks resolution time metrics. The system includes an automated knowledge base that suggests solutions before a ticket is even submitted.
